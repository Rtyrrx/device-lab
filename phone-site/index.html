How it worksModels, heatmaps & limits +

Two models, one photo

MobileNetV2 is the baseline. ConvNeXt-Tiny uses a frozen pretrained backbone and a four-class head trained with extra image variations. The dataset contains 400 original images.

Evidence, not certainty

The Class Activation Map highlights positive evidence for ConvNeXt’s chosen label. It is not an object outline or proof of correctness. Confidence scores are uncalibrated.

Private by design

Models run in this browser using WebAssembly. Photos are not uploaded. Camera capture depends on the device and browser; image selection is always available.

A few limits

Only four categories are recognized. Effects such as blur and noise are simulated. A small test set and one training seed do not establish real-world reliability.
